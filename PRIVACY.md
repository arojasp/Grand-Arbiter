# Grand Arbiter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Grand Arbiter is a free app for Magic: The Gathering judges. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no server and receives no data from the app.

## Data on your device

Everything the app keeps stays on your device:

- the rules documents and the card database;
- your quick references and notes;
- your custom ban lists (names, notes and the cards in them);
- your settings (text size, theme, contrast, keep screen on, card search) and the judge call timer.

If Android's own backup is enabled on your device, your quick references, custom lists and settings are included in your Google backup, as with any app. You can turn this off in Android's settings.

## Card searches sent to Scryfall

Most card searches run on your device. Searches the app can't do offline (for example `-t:goblin`, `or`, or a set and collector number) are sent to Scryfall's search service (api.scryfall.com) to get the results:

- **By default** they are sent as you type, after a short pause. The app says so under the empty search field.
- **If you turn off** Settings › Card search › "Search Scryfall automatically", nothing is sent until you tap "Search on Scryfall".

Only the search text is sent, with the app's name in the request. Scryfall handles it under its own privacy policy: https://scryfall.com/docs/privacy

## Other network connections

The app also connects to these services to download public content. It sends nothing about you:

- **GitHub** (raw.githubusercontent.com): new versions of the rules documents.
- **Scryfall** (api.scryfall.com, data.scryfall.io, cards.scryfall.io): card data updates and card images.
- **Wizards of the Coast** (magic.wizards.com): the original release notes, when you open them.
- **Google Play**: checking for and installing app updates.

Like any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies. The app does not use it.

## Sharing

When you share a custom list, the app hands its text to Android's share menu and you choose where it goes. The app sends nothing by itself.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
