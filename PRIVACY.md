# Grand Arbiter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Grand Arbiter is a free app for Magic: The Gathering judges. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, store or share personal data.

## Data on your device

Everything the app keeps stays on your device:

- the rules documents and the card database;
- your quick references and notes;
- your settings (text size, contrast, keep screen on) and the judge call timer.

If Android's own backup is enabled on your device, your quick references and settings are included in your Google backup, as with any app. You can turn this off in Android's settings.

## Network connections

The app connects to these services only to download public data. It does not send any information about you:

- **GitHub** (raw.githubusercontent.com): new versions of the rules documents.
- **Scryfall** (api.scryfall.com, data.scryfall.io, cards.scryfall.io): card data updates and card images.
- **Google Play**: checking for and installing app updates.

Like any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
